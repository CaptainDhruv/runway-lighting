About this system

Precision approach category II / III lighting runs 900 m out from the threshold on the extended centreline, colour-coded so a pilot can judge remaining distance in low visibility. Runway lighting carries the same coding through touchdown and roll-out.

Switching to day hides the light fixtures and shows the painted surface markings instead — the PAPI stays on in both, since it operates day and night.

This is a simplified educational model, not a certified navigation aid.
