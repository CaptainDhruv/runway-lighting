Runway 09L — plan view

3000 m runway, 45 m wide. Approach system 900 m. Lateral scale exaggerated for clarity.

White — approach, centreline, touchdown zone, edge
Red — side rows, last 300 m, runway end
Green — threshold, wing bars, taxiway centreline
Yellow — edge lights in the last 600 m
Blue — taxiway edge
Red and white — 900 m to 300 m remaining

Spacings follow ICAO Annex 14 in principle — approach centreline barrettes every 30 m, crossbars at 150 m and 300 m, red side rows over the inner 270 m, touchdown zone barrettes every 30 m to 900 m, edge lights every 60 m, centreline lights every 15 m. Counts on screen are thinned so individual fixtures stay readable.
